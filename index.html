ConversationFit

Hearing in conversation, simply better.

A small research project building a privacy-first mobile app that helps people get more out of their hearing aids in everyday conversations.

In development · public release in preparation

What we are working on

Many situations remain difficult for hearing aid users even after a careful fitting: noisy restaurants, lively group conversations, calls in busy places. ConversationFit explores how a privacy-respecting smartphone app can help people fine-tune their devices for the situations that matter most to them, without sending personal audio to the cloud.

The project runs over the funded development period and concludes with a working prototype, an open source code release and a public project report.

Open source

The complete source code of the prototype will be released under a permissive open source licence at the end of the funding period. A placeholder page is already reserved at /code/ and will later point to the public repository.
